---
title: Garden green shakshuka
date: 2026-08-15
image: ""
category: Breakfast
summary: Chard, herbs and eggs in one pan.
prep: 10 min
cook: 25 min
servings: 2
ingredients:
  - 1 bunch chard
  - 4 eggs
  - 1 onion
  - Herbs, feta, olive oil
steps:
  - Soften the onion in olive oil.
  - Add chopped chard and cook until wilted.
  - Make wells, crack in the eggs, cover until just set.
  - Finish with herbs and feta.
