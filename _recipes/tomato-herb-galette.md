---
title: Roasted tomato & herb galette
date: 2026-09-01
image: ""
category: From the garden
summary: Flaky crust, ricotta and the last tomatoes of summer.
prep: 20 min
cook: 45 min
servings: 6
ingredients:
  - 1 disc of pie dough
  - 500 g ripe tomatoes
  - 120 g ricotta
  - 1 handful basil and thyme
  - Olive oil, salt, pepper
steps:
  - Slice and salt the tomatoes; drain 15 minutes.
  - Roll the dough and spread the ricotta, leaving a border.
  - Layer tomatoes and herbs, then fold the edges over.
  - Bake at 200 °C until deep golden, about 45 minutes.
