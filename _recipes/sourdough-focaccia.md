---
title: Sourdough focaccia
date: 2026-07-20
image: ""
category: Bread
summary: Rosemary, sea salt and a long, slow rise.
prep: 30 min
cook: 25 min
servings: 8
ingredients:
  - 500 g bread flour
  - 400 g water
  - 100 g active starter
  - 10 g salt
  - Olive oil, rosemary, flaky salt
steps:
  - Mix everything and rest 30 minutes.
  - Do four sets of stretch-and-folds over two hours.
  - Oil a pan, add the dough and refrigerate overnight.
  - Dimple, top and bake at 230 °C for 25 minutes.
